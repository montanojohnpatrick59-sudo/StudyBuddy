PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy & safety

Prototype notice: this describes the current local demo, not a reviewed policy for a public service.

What this demo stores

Profile fields, group information, sessions, and discussion posts are saved in browser local storage on the device where it is used.

Where information goes

The demo does not send application data to a StudyBuddy server. Fonts and icons may load from third-party CDNs.

Safety

Do not enter passwords, sensitive personal information, or private records. Local browser data may be accessible to others who use the same browser profile.

For a public launch

Implement authentication, database permissions, deletion controls, safeguards, and a reviewed policy that accurately reflects the live service and applicable law.
